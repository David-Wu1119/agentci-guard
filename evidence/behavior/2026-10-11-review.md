# Source-provenance candidate: development-only behavior review

Compare the two clean-commit runs with:

```bash
node scripts/benchmark/report-behavior.mjs --compare \
  evidence/behavior/2026-10-11-5c045e0/cases.json \
  evidence/behavior/2026-10-11-b3f9680/cases.json
```

Both reports scanned the same 152-case v3 manifest without scan failures.
Forty cases changed: critical findings went from 6 to 32, high from 68 to 45,
and medium stayed at 184. These are scanner outputs, **not** precision, recall,
or confirmed vulnerabilities. The v3 cases were used during development.

The `ai-027` development snapshot is a candidate blind spot: its workflow
allows `Bash(gh issue:*)`, tells Claude to look up the Issue by number, and
grants `issues: write` while allowing non-write users. The new scanner drops its
critical source-to-write finding and reports `analysis_complete=true`. This
suggests an unmodeled tool-mediated fetch, but the actual Action/tool behavior
has not been verified for that workflow version. Do not count it as a confirmed
false negative or claim the removed finding was correct.

The next semantic check is to trace that exact version's Issue fetch path. If
the path cannot be closed statically, a narrow incompleteness diagnostic is
more honest than inventing a critical finding from prompt prose.
